Understanding our cancellation and refund policies

Trip Cancellation Policy for Sons of Cauvery

Last Updated: April 2025

1. Cancellation by Customer

We understand that sometimes circumstances may require you to cancel your fishing trip. Please note our cancellation policy:

  • 60 days before trip start date: Full refund
  • 45 days before the trip start date: 90% refund of the total amount
  • Between 45 and 30 days of trip start date: 50% refund of the total amount
  • Less than 30 days before trip start date: 25% refund of the total amount
  • In last 14 days before trip start date: No refund available

2. Cancellation by Sons of Cauvery

We reserve the right to cancel trips due to:

  • Unsafe weather conditions (heavy rain, flooding, storms)
  • River conditions (high water levels, strong currents)
  • Government restrictions or lockdowns
  • Equipment failure or safety concerns
  • Insufficient bookings (minimum 2 participants required)

In case of cancellation by us, you will receive a full refund or the option to reschedule at no additional cost.

3. Rescheduling Policy

We allow rescheduling of trips under the following conditions:

  • More than 7 days notice: Free rescheduling
  • Less than 3 days notice: 50% of Advance Payment as rescheduling fee

Rescheduling is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed during peak season.

4. Refund Process

Refunds will be processed as follows:

  • Online bookings: Refund to original payment method within 5-7 business days
  • Cash/UPI payments: Bank transfer within 3-5 business days
  • Processing fees are non-refundable
  • Refund confirmation will be sent via email/SMS

5. Group Bookings

For group bookings (5+ participants):

  • Cancellation must be made by the group leader
  • Individual cancellations within a group are subject to the same policy
  • Group discounts may be adjusted if group size reduces significantly

6. Emergency Cancellations

In case of genuine emergencies (medical, family emergency, etc.):

  • Contact us immediately with supporting documentation
  • We may offer partial refunds on a case-by-case basis
  • Medical certificates or other proof may be required
